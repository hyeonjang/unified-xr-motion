\section{EVALUATION}\label{sec:evaluation}
We evaluate MotionDAG along two complementary dimensions: authoring usability and system-level performance. 
First, we conduct a controlled user study to assess whether MotionDAG improves authoring efficiency and workload compared to an industry-representative workflow. Second, we analyze network offloading performance by measuring interaction latency under client-only and server-offloaded configurations.
